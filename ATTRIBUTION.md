## Additional area names (v2.0.0)

Dhaka Metropolitan and Chattogram Metropolitan police-station (thana) names, plus a few city areas for Gazipur and Narayanganj, are added in code (`includes/class-rar-wcc-locations.php`). They are public administrative place names and can be switched off under Settings → Address & Fields.
